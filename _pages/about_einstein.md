[cite_start]I am a PhD Candidate in Artificial Intelligence at the [Universitat de València](https://www.uv.es/), working within the **HiPIS (High Performance and Intelligent Systems)** research group[cite: 28, 35]. [cite_start]My research focuses on transforming Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS) from solitary experiences into **collaborative learning environments** using Large Language Models (LLMs) and Multi-Agent Systems[cite: 30, 34].

[cite_start]My core research objective is to engineer **Virtual Students**—AI agents capable of simulating peer interactions to foster collaborative problem-solving[cite: 65, 389]. [cite_start]This work is currently supported by a predoctoral contract from the **Generalitat Valenciana**[cite: 67].

Prior to my PhD, I worked as a **Full-Stack Research Engineer** and Data Scientist. [cite_start]Unlike many researchers who focus solely on theory, I have extensive experience building production-grade infrastructure using **React, Spring Boot, and Kubernetes**[cite: 110, 144]. [cite_start]I led the architectural modernization of *Tutorchat* [cite: 166] [cite_start]and developed urban air quality monitoring systems for the Valencia City Council[cite: 110, 347].
