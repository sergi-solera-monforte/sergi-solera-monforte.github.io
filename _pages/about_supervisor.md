**Miguel Arevalillo Herráez** is a **Full Professor (Catedrático)** of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the [Universitat de València](https://www.uv.es/) and a senior leader within the **HiPIS (High Performance and Intelligent Systems)** research group.

With an extensive research track record, he leads the scientific line on **Intelligent Tutoring Systems (ITS)** and **Affective Computing**. His work focuses on integrating advanced AI architectures—ranging from Computer Vision to Large Language Models—into educational platforms to personalize learning and detect student emotions.

**Key Highlights:**
* **Research Impact:** Author of over **50 JCR-indexed publications** (majority Q1) and Principal Investigator (IP) of 7 competitive research projects, including National Plan grants (Mineco/Ministry of Science).
* **Industry Transfer:** He combines academic rigor with industrial application, having led technology transfer contracts with major corporations like **EADS Astrium (Airbus)** and new-tech ventures.
* **International Experience:** Former Senior Lecturer at **Liverpool John Moores University** (UK), bringing a strong international perspective to his mentorship.

As my **Thesis Director**, Prof. Arevalillo guides my research on the intersection of **Generative AI** and **Collaborative Learning**, specifically the "Be My Mate" framework for virtual student simulation.
